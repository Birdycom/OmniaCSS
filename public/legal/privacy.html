    Privacy policy

    omniacss.eu collects as little as possible: no analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies, no third-party fonts or scripts. Last updated: 27 October 2026.

    The controller is Serge Cools, trading as Birdycom, Alexander Franckstraat 209/3, 2530 Boechout, Belgium, enterprise number and VAT BE 0633.456.916. Questions about privacy: support@omniacss.eu. This policy covers omniacss.eu. For omniaui.eu and purchases of OmniaUI Pro, see the OmniaUI privacy policy.

    2. Visiting omniacss.eu

    • Server logs. Like every web server, our hosting provider records technical data for each request: IP address, date and time, the page requested, browser type and the referring page. We need this to deliver the pages and to keep the server secure (legitimate interest). The logs are kept by our host for a limited time and are not used to profile you.
    • Theme preference. If you pick light or dark mode, your browser remembers that choice locally (local storage). It is never sent to us. See the cookie policy.
    • Nothing from third parties. Pages, fonts and scripts all come from our own server. We use no analytics, advertising or social media trackers.

    If you write to support@omniacss.eu, we use your email address, your name and your message to answer you (legitimate interest). We keep the conversation as long as needed for that.

    4. Who receives data

    Only our hosting provider (Neostrada / Totaalholding, the Netherlands) for the website and email. We never sell your data and never share it for advertising.

    5. How long we keep data

    Server logs: the short period our host keeps them. Email: as long as needed to answer you.

    6. Your rights

    You may ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, and you may object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Mail support@omniacss.eu; we answer within one month. You may also file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority: gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be.

    We update this policy when what we do changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
