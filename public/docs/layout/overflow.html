    Overflow

    _overflow.scss 0 KB JavaScriptNewest CSSv0.2.0

    Control how content behaves when it overflows an element's dimensions.

    What it does: overflow decides what happens to content that does not fit its box: show it (visible), cut it (hidden, clip) or let people scroll (auto, scroll). The -x and -y variants set one axis, and overscroll-* controls scroll chaining. When to use: scroll panels, rounded image crops, truncation. Gotchas: any value other than visible/clip on one axis turns the other visible into auto; hidden still allows programmatic scrolling while clip does not; overflow on an ancestor breaks position: sticky. See MDN: overflow and MDN: overscroll-behavior.

    ClassValue
    .overflow-autoauto
    .overflow-hiddenhidden
    .overflow-scrollscroll
    .overflow-visiblevisible
    .overflow-clipclip

    Axis Control

    Use .overflow-x-* or .overflow-y-* for specific axes.

    Overscroll: keep scroll inside the panel
