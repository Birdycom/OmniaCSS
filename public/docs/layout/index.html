    Layout

    0 KB JavaScriptNewest CSSv0.2.0

    Core utilities for controlling document flow, element display, and layering.

    The Layout section covers how boxes are displayed, how they flow, how they stack and what happens when content does not fit. Below is every page of the section with a small live example of what it does; click a title to open the page.

    Display
    How a box takes part in the layout: .block, .inline-block, .none, .contents, .flow-root.
    Text .inline-block stays in the line of text..block takes its own line
    Box Sizing
    Whether padding and border count in the width: .box-border or .box-content.
    .box-border: 9rem
    .box-content: wider
    Document Flow
    Floats and newspaper columns: .float-start, .columns-2, .columns-sm.
    The text flows around the green box, which floats to the start.
    Layering
    Which box paints on top: .z-0 to .z-50 and .isolate.
    .z-30
    .z-20
    .z-10
    Overflow
    What happens to content that does not fit: .overflow-hidden, .overflow-y-auto, .overscroll-contain.

    Backgrounds
    Background images and patterns: repeat, position, size, clip and origin, such as .bg-repeat.
    Layout Extras
    Inset, logical sizes, flex-flow, named grid areas, zoom, masks and more, such as .absolute .inset-2.
