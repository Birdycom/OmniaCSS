    Blend Modes

    _effects_advanced.scss 0 KB JavaScriptBaselinev0.2.0

    Control how an element's content blends with its background using .mix-blend-{mode}, and how an element's own background layers blend with .bg-blend-{mode}.

    mix-blend-mode: all 16 modes

    What it does: mix-blend-mode blends an element with whatever is painted behind it (siblings and the parent's background), like layer modes in an image editor. When to use: duotone images, text knocked out of a photo, glows (screen), shadows (multiply), highlight effects (difference). Gotchas: blending reaches every ancestor layer up to the nearest stacking context, so wrap the group in .isolate to keep it contained; the result depends on both layers, so always check light and dark themes; and blending can hurt text contrast. See MDN: mix-blend-mode. In every card the gradient circle is blended over three colored stripes.

    background-blend-mode: layers of one element

    What it does: background-blend-mode blends the background layers of a single element with each other and with its background-color, no extra markup needed. When to use: tinting an image with a brand color (multiply, overlay), duotone hero backgrounds, textured gradients. Gotchas: it never looks at the content behind the element, that is what mix-blend-mode is for; and it needs at least two layers (color + image/gradient). See MDN: background-blend-mode. Each card has a blue background color with a gradient and a white spot on top.
