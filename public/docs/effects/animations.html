    Keyframe Animations

    _motion_transforms.scss 0 KB JavaScriptBaselinev0.2.0

    Standard motion patterns for loaders, notifications, and interaction feedback.

    What it does: the .animate-* classes start a ready-made @keyframes animation: spin rotates forever (loaders), ping scales and fades out (notification dots), pulse breathes the opacity (skeletons) and bounce hops vertically (scroll hints). When to use: status feedback and loading states, not decoration. Gotchas: infinite animations are distracting and costly, so use them sparingly; animations of transform and opacity are cheapest. Reduced motion: when the visitor asks for reduced motion, the OmniaCSS base styles cut every animation to one cycle of 0.01ms, so a spinner stands still: always give a loader a text such as "Saving…" too. For custom timing, delays and fill modes see Animation Properties. See MDN: animation.

    ClassCSS
    .animate-spinanimation: oc-spin 1s linear infinite (a full turn)
    .animate-pinganimation: oc-ping 1s cubic-bezier(0, 0, 0.2, 1) infinite (scale to 2 and fade out)
    .animate-pulseanimation: oc-pulse 2s cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.6, 1) infinite (opacity to 0.5 and back)
    .animate-bounceanimation: oc-bounce 1s infinite (up 25% of its height and back)
    .animate-floatanimation: oc-float 6s ease-in-out infinite alternate (to translate --oc-float-x 2rem / --oc-float-y -1.5rem, scale --oc-float-scale 1.15; duration --oc-float-duration)
    .animate-typingwidth from 0 to --oc-typing-chars (20) × 1ch in steps(--oc-typing-chars), alternate over --oc-typing-duration (3.5s), plus a blinking 0.6ch end border as the caret; nowrap, overflow hidden
    .animate-waveanimation-name: oc-wave; 1.4s ease-in-out infinite as longhands (a jump of --oc-wave-height -0.35em in --oc-wave-color, then a rest)
    .animate-beatanimation-name: oc-beat; 1s ease-in-out infinite as longhands (scale 0.5 to 1.1 and opacity 0.4 to 1)
    .animate-noneanimation: none

    The four presets

    Each tile runs one class on a plain shape. The Pause button stops them all (it does what .animate-paused does), so you can look at a single frame.

    animate-spin, animate-ping, animate-pulse, animate-bounce

    The animations run on their own.
    .animate-spin
    Turns round and round, for loaders.
    .animate-ping
    A copy grows and fades, for new messages or a live status.
    .animate-pulse
    Fades in and out, for skeletons while loading.
    .animate-bounce
    Jumps up and down, for scroll hints.

    Showcase presets

    Four presets for livelier pages, each tuned with variables. .animate-wave and .animate-beat are written as longhands, so .animate-delay-stagger can give every child its own delay (without sibling-index(), in Firefox, they move together). See them in larger examples on Creative CSS.

    animate-float, animate-typing, animate-wave, animate-beat

    The animations run on their own.
    .animate-float
    Drifts up and to the right, grows a little, and back.
    .animate-typing
    Types the line one character at a time, then deletes it.
    Order 1 is ready
    .animate-wave .animate-delay-stagger
    Each letter jumps and lights up a little after the one before.
    Hello
    .animate-beat .animate-delay-stagger
    The dots grow and fade one after another.
