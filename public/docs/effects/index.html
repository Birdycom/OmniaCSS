    Effects & Motion

    0 KB JavaScriptBaselinev0.2.0

    Reference for opacity, box-shadow, blur, filters, backdrop filters, blend modes, transitions, transforms, and animations.

    All effects classes are generated from SCSS maps and available in omnia_main.css.

    Source FileUtilities Provided
    _effects.scssOpacity, box-shadow, mix-blend-mode
    _effects_advanced.scssBackdrop filter
    _filters.scssBlur, brightness, contrast, grayscale, etc.
    _motion_transforms.scssTransition, transform, animation

    All pages in this section

    Every page of the Effects & Motion module.

    PageCovers
    OpacityTransparency with .opacity-{value}.
    Box ShadowsElevation effects with .shadow-{size} (and text shadows).
    FiltersBlur, brightness, contrast, grayscale and backdrop filters.
    Blend Modesmix-blend-mode and background-blend-mode utilities.
    TransitionsAnimate state changes with .transition-*, .duration-*, .ease-*, .delay-*.
    TransformsScale, rotate and translate with the individual transform properties.
    AnimationsReady-made keyframe animations such as .animate-spin and .animate-pulse.
    Animation Properties & Motion PathAnimation longhands, linear() easing, motion path and timeline longhands.
