    Padding — what it does (box model)

    Padding is the space inside the border, between the border and the content: the background extends into it. The green area is the padding, blue is the content, like the DevTools box-model diagram.

    What it does: padding is the space inside an element, between its border and its content; the element's background paints through it. When to use: buttons, cards, inputs, any container whose content should not touch its edge. Gotchas: padding adds to the element's size unless box-sizing: border-box is on (OmniaCSS' reset sets it, see Box sizing); padding cannot be negative or auto; percentages are relative to the parent's width, even for top and bottom. See MDN: padding.

    One axis, one side

    .ph-* (horizontal), .pv-* (vertical) and the side variants .pt-* / .pb-* let you pad asymmetrically.

    content
    top and bottom
    .pt-2
    content
    top only
    .pb-2

    <button class="ph-2 pv-1">Standard Button</button>
