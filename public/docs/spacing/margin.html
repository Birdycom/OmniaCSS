    Utilities for controlling the margin (outer space) of an element.

    Common Utilities

    Class GroupValues
    .m-{n}All four sides. {n} is the spacing scale: 0, px, 0.1 … 10 in rem (m-1 = 1rem = 16px). See the spacing scale.
    .mt-{n} .mr-{n} .mb-{n} .ml-{n}One side: top, right, bottom, left
    .mh-{n}Horizontal (left & right)
    .mv-{n}Vertical (top & bottom)
    .m-auto .mh-auto .mv-autoAuto margins: center a block, or take up the free space
    .mt-auto .mr-auto .mb-auto .ml-autoAuto on one side: push the element away (to the bottom, left, top, right)
    .m-inline-{n} .m-inline-start-{n} .m-inline-end-{n}Logical: follows the reading direction (margin-inline-*), also with -auto
    .m-block-{n} .m-block-start-{n} .m-block-end-{n}Logical: top / bottom in horizontal writing (margin-block-*)
    Negative marginsNo classes: write them in your own CSS, for example margin-top: -1rem

    Margin — what it does

    Margin is the space outside the element. It pushes the element away from its container and its neighbours, and it never gets the element's background. The blue element has m-3: the orange band between it and the dashed container is 3rem (48px) on every side.

    Margin — what it does

    margin (outside the element)the element
    .m-3
    The orange band between the element and the dashed container is its 3rem margin, on every side.
    element
    margin 3remmargin 3rem3rem3rem
    1
    <div class="m-3">element</div>

    What it does: margin sets the space outside an element's border. It pushes neighbours away and never gets the element's background. When to use: spacing between siblings, centering a block with .mh-auto, nudging a single element. For spacing between many children, gap on a flex or grid parent is usually cleaner. Gotchas: vertical margins of adjacent blocks collapse (the larger one wins instead of adding up), margins of a first/last child can leak through a parent without padding or border, and auto only centers when the element has a width. See MDN: margin and margin collapsing.

    Auto margins: center and push

    auto takes all the free space. Two equal auto margins center an element; one auto margin pushes it to the other side. The orange area is exactly the auto margin. A block needs a width smaller than its container, otherwise there is nothing to share.

    Space between stacked items

    A margin on each item spaces a list, but the last item keeps its margin too. gap on the parent puts space only between the items, which is usually what you want. Gap has its own utilities on the Gap page.

    Gotcha: vertical margins collapse

    The first box has mb-2 (2rem) and the second has mt-1 (1rem). In normal block flow the two margins merge and the larger one wins: the gap is 2rem, not 3rem. In a flex or grid parent margins never collapse, so there they add up to 3rem.
