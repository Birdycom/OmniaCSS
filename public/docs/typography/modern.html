    Modern Typography

    CSS class _typography_modern.scss modern.html 0 KB JavaScriptNewest CSSv0.3.0

    Newer typography features: trim the extra space above and below text with text-box, drop caps with initial-letter, emphasis marks, CJK spacing control and font rendering switches. These complement the Advanced utilities (line clamp, text-wrap, hyphens). See MDN: text-box.

    Text box trim

    Removes the half-leading above the cap height and below the baseline, so text is optically centered in buttons and badges.

    ClassCSS
    .text-box-normaltext-box: normal
    .text-box-trimtext-box: trim-both cap alphabetic
    .text-box-trim-starttext-box-trim: trim-start; text-box-edge: cap alphabetic
    .text-box-trim-endtext-box-trim: trim-end; text-box-edge: cap alphabetic
    .text-box-trim-extext-box: trim-both ex alphabetic

    text-box-normal vs text-box-trim

    .text-box-normal
    The box keeps the space above and below the letters.

    The space above the capitals and below the baseline is cut away.

    Text wrap style & white space

    .text-stable keeps earlier lines stable while the user edits text. The white-space-collapse classes control space collapsing independently of wrapping (.text-balance, .text-pretty live in Advanced).

    ClassCSS
    .text-stabletext-wrap-style: stable
    .text-wrap-autotext-wrap-style: auto
    .whitespace-collapsewhite-space-collapse: collapse
    .whitespace-preservewhite-space-collapse: preserve
    .whitespace-preserve-breakswhite-space-collapse: preserve-breaks
    .whitespace-preserve-spaceswhite-space-collapse: preserve-spaces
    .whitespace-break-spaceswhite-space-collapse: break-spaces

    Each tile holds the same two source lines, with four spaces in the middle of each line. Look at what happens to those spaces and to the line break. .whitespace-preserve-spaces is not in Chrome yet (it looks like the first tile there). .text-stable and .text-wrap-auto give the same picture in every browser today: they only change how lines re-wrap while someone is typing, so they have no example.

    whitespace-collapse, preserve, preserve-breaks, break-spaces

    .whitespace-collapse
    The default: the spaces shrink to one and the line break becomes a space.

    .whitespace-preserve

    .whitespace-preserve-breaks
    The line break stays, the spaces still shrink to one.
    Order 1042 Shipped today
    .whitespace-break-spaces
    Like preserve, but a long run of spaces may also wrap to the next line.

    Initial letter

    Drop caps on the first letter of a paragraph. .initial-letter-3-2 = 3 lines tall, sunk 2 lines.

    ClassCSS
    .initial-letter-normal::first-letter { initial-letter: normal }
    .initial-letter-2::first-letter { initial-letter: 2 }
    .initial-letter-3::first-letter { initial-letter: 3 }
    .initial-letter-3-2::first-letter { initial-letter: 3 2 }

    Compare the three sizes: the number is how many lines high the first letter is. The large letter has no room of its own, so the lines next to it start right against it. In the wide tile below, variants on ::first-letter add that room (first-letter:m-inline-end-0.5), a bold weight and a color: a real drop cap with classes only.

    initial-letter-2, initial-letter-3, initial-letter-3-2 and a styled drop cap

    Not in this browser yet. Your browser does not support initial-letter: the first letter stays at text size (only the bold weight and the color of the last tile show).
    .initial-letter-2
    The first letter is two lines high.

    Company 1 packs every order by hand and ships it within 24 hours. You get an e-mail with a tracking link as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse.

    .initial-letter-3
    Three lines high; the text flows around it.

    Company 1 packs every order by hand and ships it within 24 hours. You get an e-mail with a tracking link as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse.

    .initial-letter-3-2
    Three lines high, but sunk only two lines: it sticks out above the text.

    Company 1 packs every order by hand and ships it within 24 hours. You get an e-mail with a tracking link as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse.

    Text emphasis

    Emphasis marks are small dots or circles placed above or below every letter. They come from Chinese and Japanese, which have no italics: there, marks like these stress a word. The marks work on any text, so you can also use them on English words, for example to make a short label stand out.

    ClassCSS
    .emphasis-nonetext-emphasis: none
    .emphasis-dottext-emphasis: filled dot
    .emphasis-circletext-emphasis: filled circle
    .emphasis-open-circletext-emphasis: open circle
    .emphasis-sesametext-emphasis: filled sesame
    .emphasis-triangletext-emphasis: filled triangle
    .emphasis-overtext-emphasis-position: over right
    .emphasis-undertext-emphasis-position: under right

    emphasis-dot, emphasis-sesame, emphasis-open-circle, emphasis-under

    .emphasis-dot
    A small dot above every letter.

    Only today

    .emphasis-circle
    A larger filled circle above every letter.

    Only today

    .emphasis-open-circle
    An open circle above every letter.

    Only today

    .emphasis-sesame
    A sesame-seed mark above every letter.

    Only today

    .emphasis-triangle
    A small filled triangle above every letter.

    Only today

    .emphasis-circle .emphasis-under
    Filled circles, placed under the letters.

    Only today

    Original use, in Japanese
    The marks stress 今日だけ, “only today”. In Japanese this does the job that italics or bold do in English.

    .text-spacing-trim-normaltext-spacing-trim: normal
    .text-spacing-trim-autotext-spacing-trim: auto
    .text-spacing-trim-space-alltext-spacing-trim: space-all
    .text-spacing-trim-space-firsttext-spacing-trim: space-first
    .text-autospace-normaltext-autospace: normal
    .text-autospace-autotext-autospace: auto
    .text-autospace-nonetext-autospace: no-autospace

    .hanging-lasthanging-punctuation: last

    Each row compares two classes on the same Japanese text. Top row: the brackets and the comma. Middle row: the gap between the Latin word, the number and the Japanese letters. Bottom row: where a narrow column breaks its lines. The hanging-* classes work only in Safari; Chrome and Firefox ignore them, so they have no example here.

    Font rendering & tab size

    Switches for variable font optical sizing, kerning, synthesized bold/italic, color font palettes and the width of tab characters in <pre>.

    ClassCSS
    .font-optical-autofont-optical-sizing: auto
    .font-optical-nonefont-optical-sizing: none
    .font-kerning-autofont-kerning: auto
    .font-kerning-normalfont-kerning: normal
    .font-kerning-nonefont-kerning: none
    .font-synthesis-nonefont-synthesis: none
    .font-palette-lightfont-palette: light
    .font-palette-darkfont-palette: dark
    .tab-2tab-size: 2
    .tab-4tab-size: 4
    .tab-8tab-size: 8

    Each row compares two classes on the same words. Kerning moves letter pairs such as A and V closer together (here in .font-serif, whose kerning is strong). Optical sizing lets a variable font (here Roboto Flex) draw large text thinner and tighter. .font-synthesis-none stops the browser from faking a bold or italic style that the font does not have (Cedarville Cursive has only one style). .font-palette-light and .font-palette-dark only work with a color font that ships light and dark palettes; none of the fonts OmniaCSS loads has them, so they have no example.

    font-kerning, font-optical and font-synthesis-none

    .font-kerning-normal
    Kerning is on: A and V, A and T tuck into each other. The teal bar shows how long the word is.
    AVATAR
    .font-kerning-none
    Kerning is off: every letter keeps its full box, the gaps look uneven and the bar is longer.
    AVATAR
    .font-optical-auto
    The default: at this size the font uses its large-text design, thin and tight.
    Rag 3
    .font-optical-none
    The same size drawn with the small-text design: heavier and wider.
    Rag 3
    .font-cursive .font-bold .font-italic
    The font has no bold or italic, so the browser fakes them: thicker and slanted.
    Thank you
    … + .font-synthesis-none
    No faking: the text stays in the one style the font really has.
    Thank you
