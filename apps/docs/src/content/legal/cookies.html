Cookie policy

omniacss.eu sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking. That is why you do not see a cookie banner. Last updated: 27 October 2026.

1. What omniacss.eu stores

NameTypePurposeKept
oc-themeLocal storage in your browser (not a cookie)Remembers whether you chose system, light or dark mode. Never sent to our server.Until you clear your browser data

This is strictly functional: it only does what you asked for (keep your theme). Under EU rules (the ePrivacy Directive, in Belgium article 10/2 of the law of 30 July 2018) such storage needs no consent.

All fonts and scripts come from our own server, so no other company sets cookies or sees your visit through this site.

2. Removing what is stored

You can remove the theme setting at any time by clearing the site data for omniacss.eu in your browser settings. The site then follows your system theme again.

3. Questions

Mail support@omniacss.eu. If we ever add cookies that need your consent, we will update this page and ask for your consent first.
