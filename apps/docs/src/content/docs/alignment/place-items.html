Place Items

_alignment.scss v0.2.0

Shorthand for align-items and justify-items: align grid items on both axes inside their cells.

In one sentence: place-items sets align-items (top to bottom) and justify-items (left to right) at once, so every grid item moves inside its own cell; in flexbox only the top-to-bottom part works.

.place-items-center is the shortest way to centre content in every cell, for example icon tiles, logos, or a single child (.grid.place-items-center). One value is used for both axes. To move the whole block of tracks instead, use .place-*. See MDN: place-items.

Real use: a logo wall

Four logos of different widths in a 2 x 2 grid with lines between the cells. With .place-items-center each logo sits in the middle of its cell.

No place-items class
Each logo stretches over its whole cell, so the marks and names hug the top-left corner.

.place-items-center
One class on the grid puts every logo in the middle of its cell, whatever its width.
