Justify Content

_alignment.scss v0.2.0

Distribute free space along the main axis of a flex or grid container using .justify-{value}.

In one sentence: justify-content moves the items along the main axis: left to right in a flex row, top to bottom in .flex-col, and the column tracks in a grid.

It decides where the items (flex) or tracks (grid) sit when they do not fill the whole container. Use it for headers and toolbars (.justify-between), centred button groups and pagination. It only has an effect when there is leftover space: with .flex-1 children or a grid of 1fr columns there is nothing to hand out. To move items on the other axis use .align-items-*. See MDN: justify-content.

Stretch needs auto-sized grid tracks

justify-content: stretch does nothing in flexbox (flex items are sized by flex-grow). In a grid it grows auto tracks, so use it with .grid-flow-col and .grid-cols-auto.

Safe alignment when the content is too wide

Plain center pushes overflowing items out at the start edge, where no scrollbar can reach them. The safe keyword falls back to start in that case. Try to scroll both rows to their first item.

Real use: header, price list and pagination

.justify-between pushes the logo and the buttons apart, and the product name and price in each row. .justify-center keeps the pagination in the middle, whatever the width.

.justify-between · .justify-center
The logo and the buttons sit at the two ends, each price at the right of its row, and the page buttons in the middle.

Gotcha: the axis follows flex-direction. With .flex-col, .justify-* works top to bottom and the container needs a height to show anything.
