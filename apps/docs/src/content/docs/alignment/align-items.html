Align Items

_alignment.scss v0.2.0

Align all children of a flex or grid container on the cross axis with .align-items-{value}.

In one sentence: align-items moves every item along the cross axis: top to bottom in a flex row, left to right in .flex-col, and up or down inside each cell in a grid.

It sets the default align-self of every child. The default is stretch, which is why flex children are equally tall. Use .align-items-center for icon + label rows and .align-items-baseline when text of different sizes must share one line. Items with a fixed height are not stretched. To move items along the main axis use .justify-*. See MDN: align-items.

Safe alignment when an item is too tall

Plain center lets a tall item stick out above the container, where no scrollbar can reach it. safe center falls back to start in that case. Try to scroll both boxes to the top line.

The item is taller than its boxCross axis ↓
.align-items-center
The item sticks out at the top and bottom. The top line is cut off and you cannot scroll up to it.

.align-items-center-safe
