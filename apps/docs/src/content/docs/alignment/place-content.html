Place Content

_alignment.scss v0.2.0

Shorthand for align-content and justify-content: position content on both axes at once.

place-content: <align> <justify> saves one class when you want the same distribution on both axes. The classic use is centring a grid or wrap block: .grid.place-center centres the tracks horizontally and vertically. It acts on tracks and lines, not on items inside cells (that is place-items), so the container must be larger than its content. See MDN: place-content.
