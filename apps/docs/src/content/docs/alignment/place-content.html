Place Content

_alignment.scss v0.2.0

Shorthand for align-content and justify-content: position content on both axes at once.

In one sentence: place-content sets align-content (top to bottom) and justify-content (left to right) in one class, so it moves the whole block of grid tracks or flex lines on both axes at once.

The classic use is centring: .grid.place-center centres the tracks horizontally and vertically. It acts on the tracks as a group, not on the items inside their cells (that is .place-items-*), so the container must be bigger than its content. A class with one word uses that value on both axes; a class with two words gives the vertical value first. See MDN: place-content.

Real use: a centred hero

A hero section of at least 17rem. .place-center puts the heading, text and buttons in the middle of it on both axes, however wide the screen is.

.grid .gap-1 .place-center
The heading, text and buttons sit in the middle of the hero, both ways.
