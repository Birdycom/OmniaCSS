Align Self

_alignment.scss v0.2.0

Override the cross-axis alignment of one single child with .align-self-{value}.

In one sentence: align-self moves one item along the cross axis (top to bottom in a flex row, left to right in .flex-col, up or down in a grid cell) and overrides the container's align-items.

It is set on the child, never on the container. Typical use: keep one button at its natural width in a card, or keep a badge at the top while everything else is centred. auto means "do what the parent's align-items says". In flexbox, an auto margin (.mt-auto) wins over align-self. See MDN: align-self.

Real use: the button in a card

Each card is a .flex.flex-col, so its cross axis runs left to right and the button stretches to the full width by default. .align-self-start or .align-self-end keeps it at its natural width. .mt-auto (a margin, not align-self) pins it to the bottom, so the buttons line up although the texts differ in length.
