Align Self

_alignment.scss v0.2.0

Override the cross-axis alignment of one single child with .align-self-{value}.

align-self lets one item opt out of the container's align-items. It is set on the child, never on the container. Typical use: push one card to the bottom of a row, or keep a badge at the top while everything else is centred. auto means "inherit from the parent's align-items". In flexbox, an auto margin (margin-top: auto) wins over align-self. See MDN: align-self.
