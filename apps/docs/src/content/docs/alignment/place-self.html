Place Self

_alignment.scss v0.2.0

Shorthand for align-self and justify-self: place one grid item inside its cell on both axes.

In one sentence: place-self moves one grid item on both axes (top to bottom and left to right) inside its own cell; put it on the child.

It sets align-self and justify-self in one class. It is the quickest way to pin one element to a corner of its cell (.place-self-end) or to centre a lone badge. For flex items only the top-to-bottom part is used. See MDN: place-self.

Real use: a badge on a product image

The image and the badge share one grid cell (.col-start-1.row-start-1 on both). The image stretches to fill the cell; place-self pins the badge to a corner or the middle, without position: absolute.
