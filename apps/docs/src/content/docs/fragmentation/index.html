Fragmentation

CSS class _layout_flow.scss fragmentation/index.html v0.3.0

Control how content breaks across pages (print) and columns: force or avoid breaks, keep a minimum number of lines together with orphans/widows, and decide how backgrounds and borders behave on a broken box with box-decoration-break. See MDN: CSS fragmentation.

Break before

Break behaviour before the element. What: break-before asks the browser to start a new page, column or region before this element (page, column) or to avoid a break (avoid). When: print stylesheets (each chapter on a new page) and multi-column layouts (a heading that starts a column). Gotcha: breaks are requests; the page-related values only matter when printing or in paged media. More: MDN: break-before.

Break inside

Prevent a card or figure from being split across columns or pages. Gotcha: avoid only works when the box fits in one fragment; a box taller than a page is split anyway. In multi-column layouts add .break-inside-avoid to every card, or they get cut in the middle. More: MDN: break-inside.

Orphans & widows

Minimum number of lines left at the bottom (orphans) or carried to the top (widows) of a page or column. When: body text in print or columns, to avoid a single stranded line (a "widow" or "orphan"). Values of 2 or 3 are typical. More: MDN: orphans.

ClassCSS
.orphans-1 … .orphans-4orphans: 1 … 4
.widows-1 … .widows-4widows: 1 … 4

Box decoration break

slice renders a broken box as one continuous box; clone gives every fragment its own padding, border and radius. .box-decoration-* are the long names, .decoration-* the short aliases. When: inline highlights that wrap over several lines (a <mark> or a tag with padding and a radius) look much better with clone. More: MDN: box-decoration-break.

Browser support

Break, orphans and widows are hints: browsers that do not support a value use their default breaking. Autoprefixer adds the -webkit- prefix for box-decoration-break.
