Responsive Overrides

Color utilities have no responsive prefix of their own; combine the color classes with the responsive display utilities (md:hidden, hidden md:block) to switch colors per breakpoint.

Small screen: blue
md and up: green
<div class="bg-blue-40 p-2 md:hidden">Small screen</div> <div class="bg-green-50 p-2 hidden md:block">md and up</div>
