Grow & Shrink

_flexbox.scss v0.2.0

Control how flex items share leftover space (grow) or give up space when the container is too small (shrink).

flex-grow distributes extra space among items in proportion to their grow factor; flex-shrink decides who gives up space when the content is too wide. By default items do not grow (0) but do shrink (1), which is why long rows squeeze before they overflow. Use .flex-shrink-0 on fixed elements such as avatars and icons, and .flex-grow on the item that should absorb the free space. A shrinking item never goes below its min-content size unless you set .min-w-0. See MDN: flex-grow and MDN: flex-shrink.

ClassBehavior
.flex-growGrow to fill empty space (flex-grow: 1)
.flex-grow-0Maintain original size (do not grow)
.flex-shrinkAllow item to shrink (flex-shrink: 1)
.flex-shrink-0Prevent item from shrinking

Flex Shorthand

Common combined grow, shrink, and basis patterns.

The shorthand sets grow, shrink and basis at once. .flex-1 (1 1 0%) ignores content size, so siblings become equally wide: ideal for equal columns. .flex-auto (1 1 auto) starts from the content width and then shares the rest. .flex-initial is the default behaviour, .flex-none freezes the item. See MDN: flex.
