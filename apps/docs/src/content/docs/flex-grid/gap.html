Gap

_gap.scss v0.2.0

Space between rows and columns of flex and grid containers.

gap adds space between items only (never at the outer edge), which removes the need for margin hacks on the first or last child. It works in grid, flexbox and multi-column layouts. .gap-x-* sets the column gap, .gap-y-* the row gap. The scale is rem based: .gap-1 is 1rem. The digit after the dot is not a decimal but a step of eighths and quarters: .gap-0.3 is 0.375rem, .gap-0.5 is 0.5rem and .gap-1.2 is 1.25rem (see the table). Gotcha: in a wrapping flexbox the row gap only appears between lines. See MDN: gap.

Real use: a wrapping row with only a column gap

A row of filter buttons with .gap-x-1 looks fine on one line. As soon as it wraps, the lines touch. Add a row gap as well, or use .gap-* for both.

Drag the corner wider or narrower and look at the space between the lines of buttons.
.flex .flex-wrap .gap-x-1
Space between the buttons, but none between the lines: they touch.
.flex .flex-wrap .gap-x-1 .gap-y-0.5
A row gap separates the lines.
