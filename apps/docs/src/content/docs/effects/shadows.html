Elevation demo — light and dark stage

The shadow utilities are light-dark() aware: in a dark color scheme the shadow alpha is much stronger and a faint light ring (plus a top highlight on .shadow-lg and up) lifts the edge of the surface, so elevation stays visible on dark backgrounds. The first demo follows the current page theme; the next two are pinned light and dark stages.
