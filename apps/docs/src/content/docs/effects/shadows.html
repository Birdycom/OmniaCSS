The scale side by side

The shadow utilities are light-dark() aware: in a dark color scheme the shadow alpha is much stronger and a faint light ring (plus a top highlight on .shadow-lg and up) lifts the edge of the surface, so elevation stays visible on dark backgrounds. This field follows the page theme: switch the theme in the header to compare.

Variables: --oc-conic-colors, --oc-conic-width (3px), --oc-conic-blur (1.5rem), --oc-conic-opacity (0.75), --oc-conic-speed (4s), --oc-conic-fill (card color). Stops with prefers-reduced-motion

.focus-visible:shadow-{none,sm,md,lg,xl}the shadow on keyboard focus

Inner shadow

.shadow-inner draws the shadow inside the box, so the surface looks pressed in: an input well, or the selected option of a segmented control.
