Blend Modes

_effects_advanced.scss v0.2.0

Control how an element's content blends with its background using .mix-blend-{mode}, and how an element's own background layers blend with .bg-blend-{mode}.

mix-blend-mode: all 16 modes

What it does: mix-blend-mode blends an element with whatever is painted behind it (siblings and the parent's background), like layer modes in an image editor. When to use: duotone images, text knocked out of a photo, glows (screen), shadows (multiply), highlight effects (difference). Gotchas: blending reaches every ancestor layer up to the nearest stacking context, so wrap the group in .isolate to keep it contained; the result depends on both layers, so always check light and dark themes; and blending can hurt text contrast. See MDN: mix-blend-mode.

In every picture the same flat magenta circle lies on top of a flat landscape, a strip of six colors and a grey ramp. Only the blend mode of the circle changes. Look at where the circle crosses the white and the black end of the ramp: that is where the modes differ most.

.mix-blend-normal
No blending: the top layer covers the one below.
.mix-blend-multiply
Multiplies the colors: the result is always darker.
.mix-blend-screen
The opposite of multiply: the result is always lighter.
.mix-blend-overlay
Multiply on dark parts and screen on light parts of the layer below.
.mix-blend-darken
Keeps the darker of the two colors.
.mix-blend-lighten
Keeps the lighter of the two colors.
.mix-blend-color-dodge
Brightens the layer below to reflect the top color.
.mix-blend-color-burn
Darkens the layer below to reflect the top color.
.mix-blend-hard-light
Multiply or screen, depending on the top color.
.mix-blend-soft-light
A softer version of hard-light.
.mix-blend-difference
Subtracts the darker color from the lighter one.
.mix-blend-exclusion
Like difference, with lower contrast.
.mix-blend-hue
Hue of the top layer, saturation and lightness of the one below.
.mix-blend-saturation
Saturation of the top layer, hue and lightness of the one below.
.mix-blend-color
Hue and saturation of the top layer, lightness of the one below.
.mix-blend-luminosity
Lightness of the top layer, hue and saturation of the one below.

background-blend-mode: layers of one element

What it does: background-blend-mode blends the background layers of a single element with each other and with its background-color, no extra markup needed. When to use: tinting an image with a brand color (multiply, overlay), duotone hero backgrounds, textured patterns. Gotchas: it never looks at the content behind the element, that is what mix-blend-mode is for; and it needs at least two layers (color + image). See MDN: background-blend-mode.

Each card is one element: an orange background-color with an image layer on top (six colors above, a grey ramp below). With .bg-blend-normal the image hides the orange completely; every other mode mixes the orange into it. .bg-blend-multiply is the classic way to tint a photo.

Practical: three everyday uses

Left: white text with .mix-blend-difference over a half white, half black panel; it turns black on the white half and stays white on the black half, so it is readable on both. Middle: a yellow highlighter on top of text; with .mix-blend-multiply the text shows through, without it the marker hides the word. Right: a logo on a black square; .mix-blend-screen drops the black, so the logo sits directly on the teal background.
