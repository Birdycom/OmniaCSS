Advanced Typography

CSS class _typography_advanced.scss advanced.html v0.2.0

Utilities for complex text behavior including line clamping, truncation, and orphans control.

Line clamp

Cut a text off after a number of lines and end it with an ellipsis (…). Handy for card previews. The three tiles hold the same text.

Classes: .line-clamp-1 … .line-clamp-6, and .line-clamp-none to show everything again. Put the class on the text itself, not on a box with padding, or the hidden lines peek out in the padding.

Hatched = room the hidden lines gave back
.line-clamp-2
Cut off after two lines, with an ellipsis.

Product 1 is a sturdy bag for everyday use. It has two outer pockets, a padded laptop sleeve and straps you can adjust. Company 1 makes it from recycled fabric and ships it within 24 hours.

.line-clamp-3
Cut off after three lines, with an ellipsis.

Product 1 is a sturdy bag for everyday use. It has two outer pockets, a padded laptop sleeve and straps you can adjust. Company 1 makes it from recycled fabric and ships it within 24 hours.

.line-clamp-none
No limit: the whole text shows.

Product 1 is a sturdy bag for everyday use. It has two outer pockets, a padded laptop sleeve and straps you can adjust. Company 1 makes it from recycled fabric and ships it within 24 hours.

<p class="line-clamp-2">Product 1 is a sturdy bag for everyday use. …</p> <p class="line-clamp-3">Product 1 is a sturdy bag for everyday use. …</p> <p class="line-clamp-none">Product 1 is a sturdy bag for everyday use. …</p>

Truncation

.truncate keeps text on one line and ends it with an ellipsis when it does not fit. Compare it with the same names without the class, which wrap onto a second line.

.truncate
Each line stays on one line and ends with an ellipsis.
Customer 1 · info@company1.example
Product 1 · Sturdy everyday bag with laptop sleeve
Order 1042 · shipped to Customer 1
without .truncate
Long lines wrap onto a second line.
Customer 1 · info@company1.example

Order 1042 · shipped to Customer 1

Balanced headings and hyphens

.text-balance spreads the words of a heading evenly over its lines, so no single word ends up alone. .text-pretty only fixes the last line, which suits paragraphs. .hyphens-auto lets the browser split long words with a hyphen in a narrow column; it needs a lang attribute and a hyphenation dictionary for that language in the browser. With .hyphens-manual you mark the break points yourself with &shy;, which works in every browser.

Long words: break-words and wrap-anywhere v0.17.3

A word that is longer than its column (a German compound in a large title, an e-mail address, a long link) runs off the screen by default. .break-words (or .wrap-break-word) breaks such a word only when it does not fit on a line of its own. .wrap-anywhere does the same, and also lets a flex or grid item shrink below the length of that word, so a narrow card stays narrow. Add .hyphens-auto and a lang attribute to get a hyphen at the break.

ClassCSS
.break-wordsoverflow-wrap: break-word
.wrap-break-wordoverflow-wrap: break-word
.wrap-anywhereoverflow-wrap: anywhere (also lowers the min-content width)
.wrap-normaloverflow-wrap: normal
default
The long word does not fit and runs past the edge of the column.
