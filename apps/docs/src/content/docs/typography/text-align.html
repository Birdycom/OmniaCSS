Text Align

Utilities for controlling the horizontal alignment of text.

ClassValue
.text-lefttext-align: left
.text-centertext-align: center
.text-righttext-align: right
.text-justifytext-align: justify
.text-starttext-align: start (left in English, right in Arabic or Hebrew)
.text-endtext-align: end

Left, center, right and justify

The same paragraph four times. Look at the edges of the lines: they line up on the left, around the middle, on the right, or on both sides (justify stretches the spaces between words).

.text-left
Lines start at the left edge.

Company 1 ships every order within 24 hours. You get an e-mail with a tracking link as soon as your parcel leaves.

.text-center
Every line is centered.

Company 1 ships every order within 24 hours. You get an e-mail with a tracking link as soon as your parcel leaves.

.text-right
Lines end at the right edge.

Company 1 ships every order within 24 hours. You get an e-mail with a tracking link as soon as your parcel leaves.

.text-justify
Lines fill the whole width; the last line stays at the start.

Company 1 ships every order within 24 hours. You get an e-mail with a tracking link as soon as your parcel leaves.

Start and end follow the writing direction

.text-start and .text-end mean "where lines begin" and "where they end". In a right-to-left page (dir="rtl") they flip on their own, so you do not need separate classes for Arabic or Hebrew.

.text-end (dir="ltr")
In left-to-right text the end is the right side.

Total: € 49.00

.text-end (dir="rtl")
In right-to-left text the end is the left side.

Total: € 49.00

<p class="text-end" dir="ltr">Total: € 49.00</p> <!-- right --> <p class="text-end" dir="rtl">Total: € 49.00</p> <!-- left -->
