Padding — what it does

Padding is the space inside the border, between the border and the content. This box has p-3: the green band is 3rem (48px) wide on every side, and the blue content starts after it.

.p-3
The green band inside the border is the 3rem padding. The blue content starts after it.

<div class="p-3">content</div>

What it does: padding is the space inside an element, between its border and its content; the element's background paints through it. When to use: buttons, cards, inputs, any container whose content should not touch its edge. Gotchas: padding adds to the element's size unless box-sizing: border-box is on (OmniaCSS' reset sets it, see Box sizing); padding cannot be negative or auto; percentages are relative to the parent's width, even for top and bottom. See MDN: padding.

Start and end: logical padding

.p-inline-start-* pads the side where a line of text starts. In a left-to-right page that is the left; in a right-to-left page (Arabic, Hebrew) it flips to the right by itself. Both boxes below have the same class.
