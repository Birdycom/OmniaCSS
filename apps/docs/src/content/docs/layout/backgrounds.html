Backgrounds

_backgrounds.scss v0.2.0

Utilities for controlling background images, gradients, and positioning.

What it does: these classes control the layers of a background: the image or gradient, its size, position, repeat, attachment and the box it is clipped or positioned to. When to use: hero images, patterns, textured panels, gradient text. Gotchas: bg-cover and bg-contain only make sense for images with an intrinsic size (a plain gradient always fills the box); bg-fixed is ignored or janky on many mobile browsers; and a background is not read by assistive technology, so keep meaningful images in <img>. See MDN: background, MDN: background-position and MDN: background-clip.

Background clip and origin

.bg-clip-* decides how far the background paints (border, padding or content box, or the glyphs of the text); .bg-origin-* decides where the 0,0 of the image sits. Here a 12px dashed border and 16px padding make the difference visible.

Gradient text with bg-clip-text

Paint a gradient as the background, clip it to the glyphs and make the text color transparent. Always keep a solid color fallback for browsers or modes where clipping fails (forced colors).
