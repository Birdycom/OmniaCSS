Backgrounds

_backgrounds.scss v0.2.0

Utilities for controlling background images, gradients, and positioning.

What it does: these classes control the layers of a background: the image or gradient, its size, position, repeat, attachment and the box it is clipped or positioned to. When to use: hero images, patterns, textured panels, gradient text. Gotchas: bg-cover and bg-contain only make sense for images with an intrinsic size (a plain gradient always fills the box); bg-fixed is ignored or janky on many mobile browsers; and a background is not read by assistive technology, so keep meaningful images in <img>. See MDN: background, MDN: background-position and MDN: background-clip.

Class GroupAvailable Utilities
Image.bg-image-none, .bg-image-gradient-*, .bg-image-radial
Size.bg-cover, .bg-contain, .bg-size-auto, .bg-size-full (100% 100%), .bg-size-x-0 (0% 100%), .bg-size-y-0 (100% 0%)

Attachment.bg-fixed, .bg-local, .bg-scroll

.bg-image-none removes a background image. There is nothing to see, so it has no example of its own: use it to switch an image off, for example at a breakpoint with md:bg-image-none.

Background clip and origin

.bg-clip-* decides how far the background paints (border, padding or content box, or the glyphs of the text); .bg-origin-* decides where the 0,0 of the image sits. Here a 12px dashed border and 16px padding make the difference visible.

.bg-clip-border
The blue runs under the dashed border too.
content
.bg-clip-padding
The blue stops at the inside of the border.
content
.bg-clip-content
Only the content area is blue; the padding stays clear.
content
<style> .clip-box { padding: 18px; border: 12px dashed var(--color-darkorange-90); background-color: var(--color-vividblue-90); color: var(--color-white-50); } </style> <div class="clip-box bg-clip-border">content</div> <div class="clip-box bg-clip-padding">content</div> <div class="clip-box bg-clip-content">content</div>

The origin moves the starting point of a background image. Here one dot is placed with .bg-no-repeat .bg-pos-left-top; only the origin class changes.

.bg-origin-border
The top-left corner of the dot is the outer corner of the border.
.bg-origin-padding
The default: the dot starts inside the border.
.bg-origin-content
The dot starts where the content starts, after the padding.

Gradient text with bg-clip-text

Paint a gradient as the background, clip it to the glyphs and make the text color transparent. Always keep a solid color fallback for browsers or modes where clipping fails (forced colors).

.bg-clip-text
The gradient is painted only inside the letters.
Gradient text

A fill that grows: bg-size-x-0 and bg-size-full

background-size can be animated. Start a one-color gradient at .bg-size-x-0 (no width) or .bg-size-y-0 (no height) and grow it to .bg-size-full on hover. Clipped to outlined text it fills the letters; on a button or link it becomes a sweep. Hover a word.

.bg-size-x-0 hover:bg-size-full
Hover the word: the fill grows from the left.
Company 1
.bg-size-y-0 hover:bg-size-full
Hover the word: the fill rises from the bottom.
