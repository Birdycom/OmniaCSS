Border Sides & Images

CSS class _borders_extended.scss sides-images.html v0.3.0

Style each side of a border separately, use logical sides that follow the writing direction, round single corners and paint borders with images or gradients (border-image). Widths per side (.border-t*) and radius presets (.round-*) are on the Border-width and Border-radius pages. See MDN: border-image.

What it does: this page covers the less common border tools: per-side style and color, logical sides (border-s-* follow the writing direction, so they flip in right-to-left languages), per-corner and logical radii, and border-image for gradient or striped borders. When to use: callouts with an accent side, RTL-ready designs, gradient borders. Gotchas: border-image ignores border-radius (use a background gradient on a padded wrapper for rounded gradient borders); logical properties need a consistent dir; and per-side colors meet at diagonal joins. See MDN: border-image and MDN: border-inline-start.

Browser support

Per-side and logical borders and border-image are broadly supported.
